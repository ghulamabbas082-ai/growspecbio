Product & Project Questions

FarmCube Nano application, operation and installation questions.

Practical answers for hospitality, retail and residential project planning.

Where can FarmCube Nano be installed?View answer

FarmCube Nano is designed for restaurants, open kitchens, cafés, hotels, airport lounges, supermarkets, food halls and premium residential interiors. Final placement should provide suitable power, service access, water management and enough surrounding space for cleaning and crop handling.

What crops can FarmCube Nano grow?View answer

The cabinet supports leafy greens, culinary herbs and microgreens through interchangeable leafy-green and microgreen tray formats. Crop selection should be planned around the available growing height, harvest target, menu use and local operating conditions.

Can FarmCube Nano be used in front of customers or guests?View answer

Yes. The enclosed cabinet and transparent crop display are intended for guest-facing environments. Restaurants, hotels, cafés and retailers can use the growing process as part of the food experience while keeping routine operation organized inside one cabinet.

How does the aeroponic system manage water?View answer

Water and nutrients are delivered to the root zone through a recirculating aeroponic system and returned to the 80 L reservoir. This closed-loop approach is designed to reduce routine water loss and keep root-zone delivery controlled.

Does operation require a trained grower?View answer

The touchscreen, crop programs and automated schedules simplify routine operation, but staff still need clear procedures for seeding, transplanting, harvesting, sanitation, water management and crop checks. Training should be included in the operating plan.

What should be confirmed before installation?View answer

Confirm the 1,500 × 687 × 2,080 mm equipment footprint, 110–277 V power supply, water filling method, floor loading, access route, drainage and cleaning workflow. The final site review should also consider guest circulation, local food-safety requirements and maintenance access.

Can FarmCube Nano support restaurant microgreens farming?

Yes. FarmCube Nano provides four controlled growing tiers for restaurant microgreens farming, alongside culinary herbs and leafy greens. Crop schedules should be planned around menu demand, harvest timing, staff routines and the selected tray format.
