FarmCube Neo · Compact 2-tier and 5-tier grow cabinets

FarmCube Neo is an automated indoor microgreen growing system designed for restaurants, hotels, cafés, commercial kitchens and premium food retail. Its compact 2-tier and 5-tier grow cabinets combine aeroponic irrigation, LED grow lights and controlled airflow to produce fresh microgreens and culinary herbs close to point of use.

WHY GROW MICROGREENS ON SITE?

Bring fresh crop production closer to preparation, service and display.

For foodservice teams, the value is not only the cabinet. It is the ability to plan a small, visible crop supply around daily menu use.

01

Freshness at point of use

Harvest microgreens and culinary herbs close to plating, garnish preparation or retail display.

02

Lower handling loss

Shorten the time between harvest and use, helping teams manage delicate crops with less avoidable shrink.

03

More stable availability

Use staggered batches and repeatable crop programmes to support a more predictable ingredient flow.

04

Menu differentiation

Grow distinctive colours, textures and flavours that can become part of a chef-led food story.

05

Customer experience

Place the cabinet where growing can be seen, without turning the dining or retail space into an exposed grow room.

INSTALLATION, CLEANING & MAINTENANCE

Confirm utilities and operating responsibilities before the cabinet arrives.

A professional installation plan should cover the selected model, access route, ventilation around the unit, food-safety procedure and the team responsible for crop handling.

01

Electrical supply

AC 110 V, 220 V or 277 V at 50/60 Hz. Confirm plug, protection and local compliance before production.

02

Water and drainage

DN20 supply and DN20 drainage are listed in the technical specification. Confirm connection positions and service access.

03

Placement

Neo-2T is suited to supported counter or compact placements; Neo-5T requires a stable floor position and clear operating access.

04

Reservoir top-up

Check water level, nutrient condition and crop programme as part of the daily operating routine.

05

Cleaning schedule

Clean trays and crop-contact parts at batch change. Sanitize the reservoir, lines and cabinet interior according to the approved site SOP.

06

Training and support

Define commissioning, crop-program setup, operator training, spare parts and remote support in the final project scope.

Operating Workflow

Load, grow, harvest, clean and restart the next batch.

The punnet-based workflow is designed to make crop handling visible and repeatable for foodservice teams.

1. Prepare and Load

Add the growing mat and seeds to each punnet, then arrange the punnets inside the 12-position crop tray.

2. Run the Crop Programme

Select the appropriate lighting schedule while the cabinet manages aeroponic misting, air exchange and the controlled growing environment.

3. Harvest and Replace

Harvest at the required crop stage, remove the used punnet and reload the tray to begin the next production cycle.

FarmCube Neo FAQ

Commercial microgreens questions for restaurant, hotel and retail projects.

What is the best commercial microgreens growing system for a restaurant?

The best system depends on available space, menu demand, crop mix and operating workflow. FarmCube Neo is designed for restaurants that need an enclosed, automated cabinet with aeroponic misting, LED grow lights and controlled airflow. Neo-2T suits compact kitchens and cafés, while Neo-5T supports higher-capacity restaurant, hotel and retail projects.

How long do microgreens take to grow indoors?

Indoor microgreen cycles vary by crop and operating recipe. FarmCube Neo reference data includes approximately 6 days for pea shoots, 7 days for radish, 10 days for basil, 13 days for carrot and 14 days for cabbage.

How many microgreens can FarmCube Neo produce?

Neo-2T holds 24 punnets per cycle and Neo-5T holds 60. Based on the supplied reference crop table, annual output ranges from approximately 624 to 1,440 punnets for Neo-2T and 1,560 to 3,600 punnets for Neo-5T, depending on crop cycle and operating schedule.

Is FarmCube Neo hydroponic or aeroponic?

FarmCube Neo uses aeroponic misting. Plant roots receive water and nutrients through a controlled mist rather than sitting in a deep-water channel.

Does the cabinet require a water connection?

The technical specification provides DN20 water-supply and DN20 drainage connections. Final plumbing, reservoir top-up and drainage arrangements should be confirmed for the selected model and installation site.

How much electricity does FarmCube Neo use?

Neo-2T has a rated power of 120 W and Neo-5T 240 W. Actual electricity use and cost depend on the crop lighting schedule, pump operation, local electricity tariff and site conditions.

Can it grow basil and other culinary herbs?

Yes. FarmCube Neo supports microgreens and culinary herbs including basil, cilantro, parsley, arugula, kale, broccoli, cabbage, radish and pea shoots. Crop recipes should be selected according to the required harvest stage and flavour profile.

What maintenance is required?

Routine work includes reservoir and nutrient checks, crop loading, inspection, harvest, tray cleaning and sanitation of crop-contact surfaces. Reservoir, water lines and cabinet interiors should follow a documented cleaning schedule suited to the site and food-safety procedure.

Is training included?

Commissioning and operator training can be included in the project scope. The final proposal should confirm installation, crop-program setup, cleaning procedures and the level of ongoing technical support.

What is the price of FarmCube Neo?

Pricing depends on the Neo-2T or Neo-5T model, finish, electrical configuration, delivery location, installation and support scope. GROWSPEC provides a project quotation after reviewing the site and operating requirements.
