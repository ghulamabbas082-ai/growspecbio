WHO WE ARE

GROWSPEC is not a collection of isolated equipment. It is a coordinated controlled-environment agriculture platform.

We develop the growing environment as a complete operating system: crop architecture, root-zone delivery, lighting, climate, fertigation, sensing, automation and the workflow surrounding production.

A project path built around the real operating objective.
