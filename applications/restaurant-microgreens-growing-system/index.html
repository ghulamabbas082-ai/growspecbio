01 / START WITH DEMAND

Size the system from menu demand, not from the number of shelves.

A practical restaurant plan begins with how many portions need microgreens or herbs each week, which crops are used, and how frequently the kitchen wants to harvest.

Define weekly use

Estimate how many punnets or grams the kitchen uses during normal service, peak periods and special events.

Choose crop mix

Separate fast-cycle crops such as pea and radish from longer-cycle herbs such as basil so harvest timing stays predictable.

Plan staggered batches

Avoid loading every tier on the same day. Staggered batches create a more continuous ingredient flow and reduce harvest spikes.

Assign responsibility

Define who checks the reservoir, crop condition, sanitation and reload schedule. Automation supports the workflow but does not replace daily crop management.

03 / DAILY WORKFLOW

Connect growing with preparation instead of treating the cabinet as a display object.

A repeatable routine is what converts cabinet capacity into a reliable ingredient flow.

01

Load

Seed or load punnets according to the planned crop calendar.

02

Grow

Run the appropriate lighting and aeroponic programme while checking crop condition.

03

Harvest

Harvest at the required stage close to preparation, garnish or plating.

04

Clean & Reload

Remove used material, clean crop-contact surfaces and begin the next batch.

04 / SITE CHECK

Confirm utilities, access and hygiene before choosing the final position.

The best location is not simply the most visible one. It must also support operation and cleaning.

Electrical supply

Confirm the selected model and local AC supply. FarmCube Neo supports 110 V, 220 V and 277 V configurations at 50/60 Hz.

Water & drainage

The technical specification includes DN20 water-supply and DN20 drainage connections. Final routing depends on the site.

Service clearance

Allow practical access for loading, reservoir checks, sanitation and maintenance without blocking kitchen or guest circulation.

Food-safety workflow

Document crop handling, cleaning frequency, seed and media storage, and responsibility for routine checks according to local procedures.

FAQ

Restaurant microgreens planning questions.

How should a restaurant size a microgreens growing system?

Start with weekly menu demand, crop mix and harvest frequency. FarmCube Neo-2T holds 24 punnets per cycle, while Neo-5T holds 60, so model selection should follow actual service volume rather than shelf count alone.

Where should the cabinet be installed?

Choose a location close enough to preparation and harvest workflow while maintaining service access, ventilation, water and drainage planning, electrical supply, cleaning access and safe guest circulation.

Which crops can be planned for restaurant use?

FarmCube Neo supports microgreens and culinary herbs including pea shoots, radish, basil, cilantro, parsley, arugula, kale, broccoli and cabbage. Final crop recipes should be validated for the required flavour, harvest stage and menu use.
