01 / DEFINE THE ROLE

Decide whether the cabinet is production, presentation or both.

Retail projects become easier to operate when the business defines what the cabinet must contribute beyond visual interest.

Fresh-food production

Use scheduled batches to supply prepared-food counters, in-store dining or small retail packs.

Customer-facing display

Place live crops close to fresh produce or foodservice areas to make the growing process visible.

Product education

Use the cabinet to explain crop varieties, harvest timing and on-site production without creating an exposed grow room.

Multi-site standardisation

A repeatable cabinet format can simplify rollout when the same crop programme is planned across several premium retail locations.

02 / PLACEMENT

Place the cabinet where customers can see it and staff can still operate it.

High visibility only works when loading, harvesting, cleaning and utilities can be managed without disrupting shoppers or food handling.

Produce department

Position the cabinet near premium fresh produce where live growing supports the category story.

Prepared-food zone

Connect crop harvest with salads, sandwiches, bowls or chef-led counters that can use microgreens directly.

Food hall

Use an enclosed cabinet in a shared hospitality setting where appearance and customer circulation are important.

Specialty retail

Smaller formats can work in gourmet stores or premium concepts where crop volume is modest but visibility matters.

03 / CROP ROTATION

Plan rotation around sales and service demand.

FarmCube Neo supports microgreens and culinary herbs. Fast-cycle crops can support frequent turnover, while longer-cycle herbs should be scheduled separately.

FAQ

Premium retail growing questions.

How can a microgreens cabinet be used in premium retail?

It can combine small-batch crop production with a visible fresh-food feature near produce, food halls or prepared-food areas. The operating plan should still prioritize crop handling, sanitation, utilities and customer circulation.

Which FarmCube Neo model fits retail use?

Neo-2T provides 24 punnets per cycle for compact placements, while Neo-5T provides 60 punnets per cycle for higher-volume or stronger visual presence. Selection should follow expected crop turnover and available floor space.

What should retailers confirm before installation?

Confirm electrical supply, water and drainage routing, staff access, sanitation procedures, customer clearance, crop loading workflow and the relationship between the cabinet and the fresh-food display.
